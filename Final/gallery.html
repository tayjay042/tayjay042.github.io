Gallery | Burgers

The 1/2 pound stuffed burgers at Road Trip have become a signature favorite for guests looking for something bold, flavorful, and unforgettable. Each burger is handcrafted with thick, juicy beef patties packed with layers of melted cheese, savory toppings, and house-seasoned ingredients cooked directly into the center for maximum flavor in every bite. Served on toasted buns with crispy fries and a variety of house-made sauces, these burgers deliver the perfect balance of comfort food and creative flair. From classic combinations to rotating specialty creations inspired by regional roadside diners, Road Trip’s stuffed burgers are designed to satisfy hearty appetites and keep guests coming back for another round

Gallery | Friday Fish fry

Every Friday, Road Trip serves up a classic fish fry that has quickly become a weekly tradition for locals and visitors alike. Featuring hand-breaded cod fried to a perfect golden crisp, each plate is served with seasoned fries, creamy coleslaw, rye bread, and house-made tartar sauce for the ultimate comfort meal. The Friday fish fry brings together the warm, welcoming atmosphere of the bar with the kind of hearty flavors that feel both nostalgic and satisfying. Whether guests stop in after work or gather with friends for dinner and drinks, Friday nights at Road Trip are all about great food, cold drinks, and good company.

Gallery | Sunday Chicken

Sundays at Road Trip are dedicated to crispy, homestyle fried chicken made fresh and served hot every week. Marinated for flavor and fried until perfectly crunchy on the outside and tender on the inside, the chicken is paired with classic comfort sides that make every meal feel like a family gathering. The Sunday fried chicken special has become a favorite way for guests to wind down the weekend, offering a relaxed atmosphere, generous portions, and the kind of homemade flavor that keeps people coming back. Combined with cold beer, signature cocktails, and a laid-back crowd, Sundays at Road Trip feel like the perfect ending to the week.
